# Chassis CAD

Store the wheelchair chassis source model, manufacturing exports, and dimensioned drawings here.

## Recommended deliverables

- Native CAD file.
- STEP export.
- Dimensioned top, side, and front views.
- Mounting-hole coordinates for the drive, steering, battery, electronics, and sensor brackets.
- Material and thickness specification.
- Mass estimate and measured finished mass.

Check wheel clearance, steering travel, cable routing, centre of gravity, and access to the emergency stop before fabrication. No chassis CAD source or verified dimensions have been committed yet.
